Registration of an Inter Vivos Trust

We refer to the above and would like to thank you for entrusting our Firm with the establishment of your trust. We realise that you might be unfamiliar with the trust registration process and would like to brief you in an attempt to better your understanding in the process.

In order for us to prepare the trust deed and attend to the necessary documentation, kindly provide us with the following information and documentation:

  • The proposed name of the trust;
  • Details of the FOUNDER:
    • Full names and surname;
    • Copy of ID document or passport;
    • Residential address; and
    • Contact details.
  • Trustee(s) (please note that the trustees will be required to accept their appointment and will only be authorised to act once the Master of the High Court has issued the relevant Letters of Authority)
    • Full names and surname;
    • Copy of ID document or passport;
    • Residential address; and
    • Contact details.
  • Beneficiaries
    • Full names and surnames;
    • ID numbers or dates of birth;
    • Relationship to the founder(s);
    • If future children or descendants are also intended to be beneficiaries, please advise us so that the trust deed can be drafted accordingly.

For FICA purposes, we will require the necessary identification (copy of birth certificate in the event of the beneficiary being a minor) and proof of address for the relevant parties.

Once we have received the above information and documentation, we will be able to proceed with preparing the draft trust deed for your consideration. Once the trust deed and supporting documentation have been signed and completed, the application will be prepared for submission to the Master of the High Court having jurisdiction. The Master will consider the application, and once the requirements have been satisfied, issue Letters of Authority to the appointed trustees.
