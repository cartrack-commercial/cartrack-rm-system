Registration of an Inter Vivos Trust

We refer to the above and thank you for entrusting our firm with the establishment of your trust. We appreciate that the registration process may be unfamiliar to you, and set out below what we require from you and what you can expect from us.

What we need from you

In order to prepare the trust deed and attend to the necessary documentation, kindly provide us with the following:

Name of the trust
The proposed name of the trust.
Founder
Full names and surname · copy of identity document or passport · residential address · contact details.
Trustees
Full names and surname · copy of identity document or passport · residential address · contact details.
Trustees must accept their appointment, and may only act once the Master has issued the Letters of Authority.
Beneficiaries
Full names and surnames · identity numbers or dates of birth · relationship to the founder.
If future children or descendants are also intended to be beneficiaries, please tell us so the deed can be drafted accordingly.
FICA
Identification for each relevant party — a birth certificate where a beneficiary is a minor — together with proof of address.
How the process runs

Once the above has been received we prepare the draft trust deed for your consideration. The deed and supporting documentation are then signed and completed, after which the application is submitted to the Master of the High Court having jurisdiction. The Master considers the application and, once the requirements have been satisfied, issues the Letters of Authority to the appointed trustees — only from that point may the trustees act.
