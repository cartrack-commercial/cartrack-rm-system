The idea

From instruction to registration, without the mental juggling.

A single transfer lives in a dozen places at once — the OTP, SARS eFiling, the municipal rates portal, two banks' attorneys, the estate agent, the Deeds Office. The real status of the file lives in your head and your inbox. When twenty files run at once, that is where things stall: a rates figure nobody chased, a bond condition quietly expiring, guarantees that don't yet match the cancellation figures.

The Pipeline is a private app for the two of you — phone and desktop. Every matter is a live file that shows its stage, what is blocking it, and the next action with a date against it. PIN-protected per person, works even with no signal, nothing to install — it opens from a private link and sits on the home screen like any app.

1
Instruction received
File opened in seconds — property, parties, price, agent. A reference like T26/018 from day one.

OTP checked, title deed search, servitudes & interdicts, suspensive conditions diarised with deadlines.

FICA for both parties, POA and declarations drafted, signing appointments tracked to done.

SARS duty receipt, municipal rates clearance, HOA / body-corporate consent, compliance certificates.

Cancellation figures vs guarantees, deposit safe in trust — the three-attorney handshake in one view.
6

Everything signed and certified; simultaneous lodgement arranged with bond & cancellation attorneys.
7

The 7–10 working-day countdown, with exam queries logged so nothing gets rejected twice.
8
Registered
Ownership passes. Disbursements, agent commission, final statements — the registration-day runbook.
9
Settled & closed
Keys confirmed, the registered title deed delivered, file archived — and it counts on your dashboard.
The engine behind this already runs a commercial sales pipeline in daily production use — this is the same proven machine, rebuilt file-by-file around conveyancing.

Under the hood

The system watches, so you don't have to.

Each file carries a living checklist built from how a transfer really runs — and the app turns all of it into reminders, blockers and a clean picture of the practice.

The checklist brain

Every file is born with its full checklist: FICA for both parties, POA & declarations, SARS transfer duty receipt, municipal rates clearance, HOA / body-corporate consent, compliance certificates. Tick as you go — the file knows what is still missing.

Three attorneys, one heartbeat

Bond attorney and cancellation attorney tracked per file: figures requested, guarantees received and checked against cancellation figures, everyone ready for simultaneous lodgement. The coordination you carry in your head, written down.

Deadlines that police themselves

Suspensive conditions with dates, bond-approval deadlines, rates clearance expiry warnings, the exam countdown after lodgement, and a nudge when any file sits untouched too long. It all lands on My Day — nothing relies on memory.

The money picture

Deposit received in trust, guarantees in, registration-day disbursements ticked off, and your fee per file rolling into a monthly dashboard — registrations, fees, and average days from instruction to registration.

A workflow tracker alongside your trust accounting — never a replacement for it.

Registration-day runbook

The moment a file registers: settle the seller's bond, agent commission, final line-item statements to buyer and seller, keys released, registered title deed delivered. A short list that ends with a closed, archived file.

Private by design

A private link for the two of you — each with your own PIN. The data belongs to the practice, backed up daily, and the app keeps working offline in a signal dead-spot (Deeds Office basements included).

Proven engine

The same core has run a demanding commercial pipeline in daily use since early 2026 — this is not a first attempt.

Nothing to install

It opens from a private link and sits on the home screen like any app — phone, tablet and desktop alike.

Yours to keep

One-tap backup of every file, exportable any day. No subscriptions to a stranger, no lock-in.
