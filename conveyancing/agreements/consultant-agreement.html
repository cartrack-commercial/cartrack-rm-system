Note for the Director — clause 3.4

An attorney may not share professional fees with a person who is not an admitted legal practitioner. If the Consultant is not admitted, any remuneration calculated as a percentage of conveyancing fees earned on her files is likely to fall foul of that rule, and the exposure is the Director's, not the Consultant's. Clause 3.4 is drawn to keep the arrangement on the right side of it. Please settle the fee basis in clause 3.1 with that in mind.

Notes for the Director — not part of the agreement
  • This is a draft, not advice. It was prepared to give Hesca a working starting point, by someone who is not her attorney. She is the admitted practitioner and must settle it — ideally with a colleague who does employment and LPC work — before it is signed.
  • Maryke's admitted status (clause 1.3) is the hinge. Whether she is an admitted conveyancer, an admitted attorney only, or unadmitted changes clauses 1.3, 2.3.2, 2.3.4 and 3.4, and changes what title she may safely carry. Settle this first; everything else follows from it.
  • The title in clause 1.2 is deliberately blank. A title suggesting admission — "Conveyancer", "Associate", "Attorney" — is a representation to the public and to clients, and the exposure runs to the Firm. If Maryke is unadmitted, something like "Conveyancing Consultant" or "Conveyancing Manager" is safer.
  • Contractor or employee. This is drawn as an independent contractor agreement, on the strength of Maryke invoicing the Firm. If the reality is that she works the Firm's hours, at the Firm's premises, under the Firm's control, a court or SARS may find an employment relationship whatever the paper says — and the liability for PAYE, UIF and unfair dismissal would be the Firm's. Worth a candid look before signature.
  • The indemnity in clause 7.3 is currently uncapped. An uncapped personal indemnity is often resisted and is sometimes read down. Consider a cap, or reliance on the Firm's professional indemnity cover instead.
  • Annexure A (scope of services) and Annexure B (fees) still need to be drafted. Say the word and I will draft both from the nine-step transfer process already prepared.
